Try RTPL on a phone

RTPL has no app of its own. It runs inside the delivery platform and shows up in three places. Tap anything: the real RTPL rules decide what happens.

  • Rider appSees a checked time and why, reports delays, asks for review.
  • Customer appGets the same arrival time. Nothing private.
  • Reviewer consoleA person decides appeals and must give a reason.

Try these

  1. Accept, pick up, deliver: on time.
  2. Stop for 10 min, deliver, then ask for review and play the reviewer.
  3. Get stuck in traffic with no record: still protected.
  4. Road closed, then a shortcut: the time is kept.
  5. Settings → route data out of date: dispatch is held.
